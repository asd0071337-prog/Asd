    Your data, your device

    Everything is stored in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data clears the app. Use export/import to back up or move to another device.

    Currency & exchange rate

    The app displays everything in Icelandic króna. When you refresh stock prices, US-listed tickers come back in USD and get multiplied by this rate to show in ISK. Update it when the króna moves meaningfully.

    (Icelandic tickers ending in .is are treated as already in ISK.)

    AI lookup

    The screener's "Look up & analyze with AI" button uses your own Anthropic API key to call Claude with web search. Your key is stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent directly to api.anthropic.com — it never touches any other server. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

    About the scoring

    The screener uses a transparent weighted-score across value (P/E), growth, profitability (ROE), leverage (Debt/Equity), shareholder returns (dividend), cash generation, and moat. The exact weights are visible in the breakdown that appears below your result.

    Quote refresh tries a public Stooq CSV endpoint; if it's blocked by your network, the app falls back to the prices you've entered manually.
